## Purpose and market selection

Founder requires comparing Sesli Öğren's visual/learning experience to **highly downloaded learning apps** as well as **audio reading, book summaries and study-from-own-sources**. Benchmark selection is *purposeful breadth*, not a falsely precise worldwide-download ranking. Google Play rounded Android thresholds are **lifetime-ish listing thresholds, not exact installs, iOS totals, active users, revenue, UX quality or causal evidence of design success**. Larger apps may have extraordinary brand, marketing, network and distribution advantages. All numbers below checked 2026-10-08 from Google Play public listings.

## 3. Where Sesli Öğren should win (risk-adjusted, honest competitive promise)

**Do not try to beat all apps in total features, daily installs, catalog size, narrators or raw audio quality at V1.** Define **one narrow job-to-be-done**:

> “Türkçe kendi ders materyalimi getiriyorum. Dinlemekle kalmadan neyi hatırlayabildiğimi deniyorum; uygulama sonucu **kaynağımın içinde** dürüstçe gösterip, bir sonraki gerçek çalışma adımını sürdürülebilir şekilde sunuyor.”

**Four coupled differentiators to TEST, not assert as achieved:**
- **C1 — Personal-source legibility vs readers:** From Home, actual material is identity and action, not a generic book/catalog or abstract hero card. Long source reading/scroll works.
- **C2 — Active knowledge proof vs listening/summary apps:** Input answer→help/unknown distinction→literal source anchor/result→bounded one-attempt state. Listening alone is never mastery.
- **C3 — Reduced cognitive/tool-switch cost vs all-in-one labs:** Read, optionally listen, deliberately attempt, see grounded correction, return to source in one consistent visual place; aim for prepared-source first-minute utility. Check if source-only navigation is actually easier than NotebookLM, Quizlet, Speechify.
- **C4 — True return value vs noisy habit gamification:** Resume a real learner material and canonical next action; genuine unfinished learning reason. No fake streak, score, readiness or notification pressure.

**Counter-hypotheses (must actively try to disprove):**
1. NotebookLM + Quizlet already outperform both feature scope and user value; our narrow loop may feel too limited. Capture first-person comparison with own notes, never rely on “unique AI” claim.
2. Source Stage may remain a generic reader with Recall bolted on. If the physical/visual source→answer relationship is not demonstrably clearer than Quizlet/NotebookLM, STOP and redesign.
3. Speechify/ElevenReader audio ergonomics may be materially better. Our existing local TTS may need focused player UX improvements, but this is an implementation QA topic, not justification for speculative TTS service architecture.
4. Student desire can favor ease/speed and free value over 'premium'-looking UIs. Test “would actually switch?” with uncoached learners rather than asking whether screenshots are pretty.
5. Broad feature parity would overrun V1, create expensive assets/providers and delay launch; prioritise first authentic learning payoff and fail noncritical premium “nice-to-haves” into backlog.

## 4. Practical BENCHMARK GATE (new blocker for LA-0040 Q0/Q3/Q5)

Before locking the new visual direction, for top **5 direct comparators** (`Quizlet, Brilliant, NotebookLM, Speechify, Blinkist`) evaluate each at the SAME **design task** and record: actual product page/source type, first 3–5s message, first-tap affordance, 60s prepared-source/analog payoff, main source/text visual, primary interaction, return reason, free/paid clarity, accessibility signal, and recognizable visual identity. Mark `OBSERVED_PUBLIC` versus `INSTALL_VERIFIED` versus `NOT_VERIFIED` for each datapoint.

**Honest cross-category setup:** Duolingo and Brilliant do not offer our exact arbitrary PDF → Recall workflow in their stated core UX; compare engagement/feedback grammar only. Blinkist/Headway/kitUP do not hold *the student's own* textbook as their primary catalog; compare reading/listening/continuation grammar only. NotebookLM, Quizlet and Speechify are the closest task substitutes for their matching stages.

Required prototype vs market side-by-side:
- **B01 3–5s:** Sesli Öğren current material+action understood as clearly as relevant leading UI, by actual 5–8 users; non-understanding is a return condition.
- **B02 60s:** prepared source → answer submission → **verifiable source feedback**, with user comprehension; no assuming it's faster without timed uncoached test.
- **B03 visual craft:** blind real phone-sized screenshot and 5–10s interaction comparisons. Founder can independently FAIL an ugly or generic composition despite technical PASS.
- **B04 source/audio continuity:** reader→listen→reader and reader→Recall→source result→resume show real continuity; benchmark Speechify/ElevenReader player controls only to the extent currently implemented. Unsupported features are gaps, not PASS.
- **B05 desire + substitution:** ask for their actual current study workaround and observe whether they voluntarily choose our real flow over Quizlet/NotebookLM-style alternative. Record hesitations and reasons, not self-reported false certainty or made-up conversion probabilities.
- **B06 non-copied identity:** visual grammar is distinctive when brand masked and materially improves user action, not merely recoloring another app. Rights/provenance gate passes before any direct asset use.
- **B07 evidence/commercial boundaries:** first useful outcome free if approved, no fabricated paid tier; no claims of proven D7/D30 return until actual cohort exists.

**Admission / failure rule:** Any Q0 market benchmark incomplete = **BENCHMARK_INCOMPLETE**, not “ready to lock”; engineering prototype may proceed if comparisons not needed to implement, but **no Founder visual direction lock** until relevant comparable real screen evidence and core B01–B06 observation exist. A strong opponent's feature does NOT automatically enlarge Sesli Öğren's first product; admit a gap only if it blocks our core outcome.
