## Product-quality operating notes

### Whole-product coupling
Every representative surface reinforces material -> action -> evidence -> next action -> return.

### Payoff ladder
- NOW: clear next tap.
- SESSION: truthful, satisfying learning result.
- NEXT SESSION: visible unfinished next action.
- MULTI-SESSION: material-level evidence continuity without fake mastery percentages.

### Cold/fresh-player stress
Evaluate:
- what is this app for in 3 seconds?
- what should I tap in 10 seconds?
- can a prepared-material returning user reach meaningful learning within 60 seconds?
- after one result, can the learner explain what changed and what did not?
- is the reason to return concrete?
