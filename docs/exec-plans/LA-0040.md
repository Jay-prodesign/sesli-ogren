## Required methodology

### B. Whole-product coupling audit
Review:
SOURCE/MATERIAL -> CURRENT STATE -> ACTIVE ACTION -> FEEDBACK/EVIDENCE -> NEXT ACTION -> RETURN.
A beautiful isolated screen cannot compensate for a fragmented chain.

### C. Fresh-user stress
- 3–5 second: what is this and why should I care?
- 10 second: what should I do now?
- 60 second: can I reach a meaningful action and perceive payoff?
- friend-retell: can a student explain why this is different?
- replacement test: why open this instead of a generic AI/audio/study app?
