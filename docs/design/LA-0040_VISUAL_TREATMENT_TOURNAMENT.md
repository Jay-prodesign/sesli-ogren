# LA-0040 Visual Treatment Tournament

Status: LEADING PROTOTYPE LANE — T5 Visual Learning Studio × Student Momentum is the current leader, but final lock is deferred until prior roadmap/research authority is reconciled and representative runtime truth is preserved.

## Scoring
Each lane is judged 1–5 on:
student desire, clarity, identity, trust, show-don't-tell potential, payoff potential, long-session comfort, companion fit, scalability and asset burden (inverse).

## T1 — Calm Premium Scholar
Hypothesis: quiet editorial learning environment; paper-like canvas, refined type, restrained accent, sparse companion.

Strengths:
- trust and reading comfort;
- durable across subjects;
- low asset burden.

Risks:
- repeats current weakness: competent but low-desire;
- can feel adult/corporate rather than student-native;
- limited emotional payoff.

Round-0 score: **32/50**
Disposition: **HOLD as control**, not preferred.

## T2 — Energetic Student Momentum
Hypothesis: more color, stronger action contrasts, visible session momentum, bolder transitions, compact reward moments.

Strengths:
- continuation desire;
- obvious next action;
- stronger student fit;
- stronger social/store screenshot presence.

Risks:
- easy to become noisy/gamified;
- may weaken trust or long-session comfort;
- companion can become overly cartoon-driven.

Round-0 score: **38/50**
Disposition: **ADVANCE**, but must be constrained by learning truth.

## T3 — Friendly Intelligent Companion
Hypothesis: companion becomes a highly integrated guide; UI palette and shapes derive from the character’s visual language.

Strengths:
- memorability;
- warmth;
- onboarding/feedback potential;
- potentially ownable brand.

Risks:
- age can skew too young;
- expensive state/animation burden;
- character may overpower own-material learning.

Round-0 score: **35/50**
Disposition: **ADVANCE AS A COMPONENT HYPOTHESIS**, not sufficient as whole treatment alone.

## T4 — Visual Learning Studio
Hypothesis: learner’s own material becomes the visual protagonist. Concepts, source fragments, active tasks, evidence and next actions become a dynamic visual workspace; companion is secondary and functional.

Strengths:
- strongest show-don't-tell potential;
- strongest differentiation from generic chat/TTS;
- makes learning state/product value visible;
- works across serious and younger students;
- product can feel rich without fake game rewards.

Risks:
- requires careful information design;
- material-specific visuals cannot depend on bespoke art;
- complexity can grow if primitives are not disciplined.

Round-0 score: **43/50**
Disposition: **ADVANCE — CURRENT LEADER.**

## T5 — Expressive Studio × Momentum Hybrid
Hypothesis: T4 structure with selected T2 energy—calm canvas and material-led workspace, but stronger color moments, kinetic feedback and high-clarity session progression.

Strengths:
- balances study comfort with desire;
- strongest candidate for premium student product;
- allows companion to support rather than define brand;
- high store-screenshot potential.

Risks:
- could become visually inconsistent if energy is not tokenized;
- needs strict motion/asset budget;
- requires real prototype comparison, not prose selection.

Round-0 score: **45/50**
Disposition: **ADVANCE AS LEADING PROTOTYPE.** Rebuild against the priority-weighted high-school audience, canonical D/Knot authority, prior gameful/living-learning roadmap intent and real implemented feature truth; visual PASS still requires VQG-01.

## Tournament disposition
T5 is the strongest current concept, but the first comparison was a direction sketch rather than an implementation-truth mockup. Do not treat it as final authority yet. Preserve earlier Learning App experience research and D/Knot decisions, then prototype T5 using only real/current product capabilities. T1/T2 remain controls; no need to fully implement them unless T5 fails a material gate.

## Required treatment proof
For each finalist show:
- Home prepared-material state;
- Workspace;
- Recall prompt;
- Recall payoff;
- one Listen state;
- companion role;
- long-title/narrow-phone variant.

## Selection criterion
Winning lane maximizes:
**student desire × learning clarity × identity × long-session comfort × truthful payoff**
subject to accessibility and sustainable asset production.

A treatment that wins beauty but loses learning truth is rejected.
