# LA-0040 Companion Reevaluation

Status: OPEN FORM / LOCKED CONSTRAINTS — prior D/Knot visual lock is superseded; plush/cartoon mascot treatment is explicitly rejected by Founder.

## Founder correction
The round blue/purple plush humanoid companion shown in the generated LA-0040 comparison board is **REJECTED**. Do not reproduce or approximate that appearance.

## Question
Should D/Knot evolve into a sparse living graphic/signal mark, be materially redesigned under that brief, or be removed?

## Current D/Knot strengths
- memorable silhouette at medium size;
- warmth and emotional softness;
- useful feedback/state channel;
- gives the product a potentially ownable human layer.

## Current weaknesses
- glossy plush/3D rendering is visually separate from the flat, restrained UI;
- at small sizes some form detail collapses;
- may skew younger than the full student market;
- current blue/purple palette pushes the whole product toward generic “AI education” color space;
- frequent use risks mascot fatigue;
- if made central, asset/state burden grows quickly.

## Tournament candidates
### C1 — KEEP unchanged
Low implementation cost; weakest integration opportunity.
Round-0: HOLD/control.

### C2 — EVOLVE D/Knot
Preserve name/core silhouette/personality, redesign palette/rendering/proportions for:
- clearer icon-scale read;
- less plush/toy feel;
- stronger graphic/editorial quality;
- token-aligned colors;
- production-ready state family.

Round-0: **LEADER.**

### C3 — REDESIGN materially
Preserve only product role/name or conceptual DNA. Explore a more iconic abstract/graphic companion designed around learning-state communication.

Round-0: ADVANCE if C2 cannot meet student/premium gate.

### C4 — SPARSE companion
Retain D/Knot but show only in onboarding, active-thinking, result and recovery moments; remove routine decorative placement.

Round-0: **ADVANCE; likely combinable with C2/C3.**

### C5 — REMOVE
No mascot. Product identity comes entirely from material-led visual system.
Round-0: valid control, not preferred until tested.

## Recommendation before visual prototyping
Prototype **C2 + C4** first, but with a stricter brief: 2D/vector-first, abstract/simplified, teen-credible, no animal/plush/baby-face/oversized-cartoon-eye identity, and used only at meaningful guidance/result moments. Keep C3 and C5 as escape routes.

## Companion quality requirements
- recognizable at 32 px;
- no essential meaning carried by expression alone;
- neutral/think/speak/success/support states clearly separable;
- does not imply mastery;
- no state requires frame-heavy bespoke animation to function;
- appearance fits older teen/university as well as younger students;
- rendering works on light/dark-neutral surfaces;
- production rights/provenance recorded;
- Reduced Motion loses no meaning.

## Final protected decision
KEEP / EVOLVE / REDESIGN / REMOVE is a Founder visual-direction gate after representative treatment review.
