## Screen findings

### What now works
- “Kaldığın materyal” is a real object, not a generic hero.
- Real learner state is visible without fake progress.
- “Şimdi” creates momentum without inventing a daily quest system.
- Recall vs Listen is immediately legible.

- companion in the global header is acceptable, but must not become mandatory decorative wallpaper.

Severity: **MEDIUM / POLISH**, not HIGH.
